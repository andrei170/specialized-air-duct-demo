Be honest with yourself

Not every house needs its ducts cleaned. Here is how to tell.

Plenty of companies will clean anything you let them. We would rather you called us when it actually helps.

Worth doing

Call us if any of these are true

  • Visible dust pushing out of the registers when the system kicks on
  • You have just had building work, a renovation or a new floor
  • Somebody in the house has allergies that are worse indoors
  • You have moved into a house and have no idea of its history
  • There is a musty smell only when the HVAC is running
  • Your dryer takes two cycles to dry a load
Probably not yet

Save your money if

  • The ducts were properly cleaned within the last three to five years
  • The house is new and nobody has done building work since
  • You have no dust, no smell and no symptoms

The dryer vent is different — that one is worth doing on a schedule regardless, because it is a fire risk rather than an air quality one.

Dryer vents

The lint trap catches maybe half of it. The rest is in the wall.

Every load pushes lint down the duct. It builds where the run bends, the airflow drops, the dryer runs hotter and longer, and eventually you have a fire risk sitting behind your laundry room.

Straight answers

What people ask before they book

Including the question most duct cleaning companies would rather you did not ask.

Do my ducts actually need cleaning?

Sometimes the honest answer is no. If your home is new, nobody has done building work, and there is no dust, smell or allergy problem, cleaning the ducts is unlikely to change anything you would notice. We would rather tell you that than take the booking. Dryer vents are different — those are a fire risk and worth doing on a schedule regardless.

What does it cost?

It depends on the square footage, how many supply and return vents you have, and whether the system has ever been cleaned. We can usually give you a price on the phone in about two minutes once we know those three things.

How long does it take?

A typical home is a few hours. A dryer vent on its own is much quicker. Commercial buildings depend on the size of the system and we will tell you before we start.

Will it help my allergies?

It can, particularly after building work or if there is visible dust coming from the registers. It is not a cure, and anybody promising you one is overselling. What we can tell you honestly is what is actually in your ducts, because we photograph them before and after.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes — offices, restaurants, schools, apartment buildings and laundromats. We schedule around your operating hours where we can and document the work for your records.
